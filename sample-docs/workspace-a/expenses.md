# Acme Corp — Expense Policy

## Reimbursable

- Client meals up to $75 per person.
- Economy airfare; premium economy on flights over 6 hours.
- Ground transport (rideshare, transit, parking).
- Conference tickets pre-approved by your manager.

## Not reimbursable

- Alcohol on solo travel.
- Hotel room upgrades.
- Personal entertainment.

## Process

Submit receipts within 30 days via the finance portal. Reimbursement lands in
your next payroll cycle. Amounts over $500 require a second approval from
Finance.
