# Acme Corp — New Hire Onboarding

Welcome to Acme. This guide covers your first week.

## Accounts

You will receive a company email within 24 hours. Use it to sign in to the
internal portal. Two-factor authentication is mandatory from day one.

## Equipment

Laptops are issued by IT on your first morning. Standard issue is a 14" machine
with 32 GB of RAM. Requests for larger machines go through your manager.

## The launch codeword

For the Q3 product launch, the internal codeword is **ORANGE-PANGOLIN**. Do not
share it outside the launch team. It is used to gate access to the staging
environment during the embargo period.

## Time off

Paid time off accrues at 1.75 days per month. Submit requests at least two weeks
in advance through the HR portal.
